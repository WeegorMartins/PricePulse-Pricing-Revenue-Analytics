# Pricing Experiment Template

## Hypothesis

Increasing/decreasing the price of SKU X by Y% will improve the primary financial KPI without an unacceptable deterioration in secondary KPIs.

## Treatment

Selected test stores receive the candidate price.

## Control

Comparable stores retain the existing price.

## Primary KPI

Prefer an economically aligned KPI such as gross margin per store-week.

## Secondary KPIs

- Units
- Revenue
- Category revenue
- Adjacent SKU revenue
- Stockout proxy if available

## Duration

Do not hard-code duration. Determine using:
- baseline variance
- minimum detectable effect
- alpha
- statistical power
- business cycle

## Decision

Scale / iterate / reject.
