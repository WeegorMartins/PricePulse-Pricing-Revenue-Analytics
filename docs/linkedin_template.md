Opening idea:

**Increasing prices does not necessarily increase revenue.**

The more useful question is where demand appears sufficiently insensitive to price for a controlled test to make economic sense.

I built **PricePulse**, a retail Pricing & Revenue Analytics case designed around that decision.

Instead of starting with a dashboard, I structured the project around:

- historical price architecture
- price elasticity modeling
- uncertainty and robustness
- Price-Volume-Mix decomposition
- scenario simulation
- recommendation guardrails
- experiment design
